# Hackathon Rules

## What are the hackathon rules
The club hackathon runs for 36 hours. Teams are two to four RGUKT-ONGOLE students. All code must be written during the event window; pre-existing personal libraries must be declared at kickoff.

## Registration
Teams register before the kickoff session with a team name, member list, and a one-line idea. Late registration is not accepted after kickoff ends.

## What you must submit
Each team submits a public repository link, a written build log published on Builder Center, and a three-minute demo video. Submissions close at the announced deadline; late submissions are not judged.

## Judging criteria
Judging is out of 100: working demo 40, technical depth 25, clarity of the write-up 20, and originality 15. Ties are broken by the working demo score.

## Fair play
Reusing open-source components is allowed when the licence permits and the use is credited. Submitting another team's work, buying a solution, or faking a demo results in disqualification.

## Prizes and recognition
Winning teams are featured in the club newsletter and receive club certificates. Cash prizes are not guaranteed and depend on sponsorship for that edition.

## Questions during the event
Every hackathon has a duty organiser announced at kickoff. For rule disputes, the chapter lead's decision is final.
