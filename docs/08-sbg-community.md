# SBG Community

## Community channels
The club runs a campus group for announcements, a help thread for technical questions, and a submissions thread for published work. Announcements are the only channel used for official dates.

## Code of conduct
Be respectful, assume good intent, and keep feedback about the work rather than the person. Harassment, spam, and sharing other members' personal information are not tolerated and lead to removal from the club.

## Roles in the club
The chapter lead runs the club and owns final decisions. Workshop facilitators prepare and deliver sessions. Project mentors support teams during build weeks. Members can volunteer for facilitator or mentor roles each semester.

## Getting help
Ask in the help thread with what you tried, the exact error, and a code snippet. If your question involves account access or membership, contact the chapter lead directly.

## Staying active
Members are counted as active if they attend at least two workshops and publish at least one build log per semester. Active members get priority for hackathon team formation and event seats.
