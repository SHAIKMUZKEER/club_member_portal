# Onboarding FAQ

## What is the AWS Student Builder Group at RGUKT-ONGOLE
The AWS Student Builder Group (SBG) at RGUKT-ONGOLE is a student-run community club for learners who want hands-on cloud experience. Members meet for workshops, build small projects, and publish their work publicly.

## Who can join the club
Any currently enrolled RGUKT-ONGOLE student can join. No prior cloud experience is required. First-year students are welcome and are usually paired with a senior buddy for their first project.

## How do I join
Fill in the club onboarding form shared in the campus group, attend one orientation session, and complete the "hello cloud" starter task. Once the starter task is submitted, your name is added to the member roster.

## What should I bring to my first session
A laptop, a working internet connection, and a personal email address you can use for club sign-ups. Lab machines are available in limited numbers, so bringing your own laptop is recommended.

## How much time does membership take
Most members spend two to four hours a week: one weekly workshop plus project time. Hackathon weeks are heavier.

## Is there a membership fee
No. Club membership is free for all RGUKT-ONGOLE students.
