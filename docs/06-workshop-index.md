# Workshop Index

## When is the next workshop
Workshops run every Saturday at 10:00 AM in the campus seminar hall, with an online link shared the night before. The next session is always announced in the club group by Thursday evening.

## Workshop schedule for the semester
Week 1 Cloud fundamentals and account safety. Week 2 Storage and static site hosting. Week 3 Serverless functions. Week 4 Databases and APIs. Week 5 Bedrock starter track. Week 6 Build week and project clinic. Week 7 Hackathon prep. Week 8 Demo day.

## Attendance and prerequisites
Attendance is open to all members. Sessions from Week 3 onward assume you finished the account setup guide and have MFA enabled.

## Recordings and materials
Slides and lab instructions are shared in the club drive after each session. Recordings are posted for online sessions only.

## Requesting a topic
Members can request a workshop topic through the club feedback form. Topics with five or more requests are scheduled in the following month.

## Rescheduling
If a session is moved due to exams or campus events, the change is announced at least 24 hours in advance in the club group.
