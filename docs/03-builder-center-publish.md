# Publishing on AWS Builder Center

## What Builder Center is used for in the club
Builder Center is where club members publish written work: build logs, tutorials, and project write-ups. Publishing is how members show progress and how the club tracks activity for the semester.

## Creating your builder profile
Sign in with your builder ID, complete your profile with a display name, a short bio mentioning RGUKT-ONGOLE, and a profile photo. A completed profile is required before your first post is counted by the club.

## How do I publish on Builder Center
Draft your post, add a clear title and summary, tag it with the technologies you used, then publish. Copy the published link and share it in the club submissions thread so the chapter lead can record it.

## What makes a good club post
Write about something you actually built or debugged. Include the problem, the steps you took, one or two screenshots or diagrams, and what you would do differently. Aim for 600 to 1200 words.

## Publishing rules for club members
Do not publish credentials, account IDs, or private student data. Do not republish someone else's work as your own. Posts that are fully generated without your own build work are not counted toward club activity.

## Post review before publishing
If you want feedback first, share the draft link in the club review thread at least two days before you plan to publish. Reviewers usually respond within 48 hours.
